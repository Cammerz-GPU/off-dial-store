The Outlier

For people who notice the one thing that doesn’t quite belong.

Details

Originally created as an exploration of repetition, colour and imperfection, The Outlier takes the clean white dial of the Citizen Tsuyosa and gives it a less predictable personality.

The dial is built around individually hand-painted blue circular marks, each with its own texture, depth and variation. Among them sits a single red mark — deliberately different, deliberately out of place. It is a small interruption that changes the whole composition.

That contrast is the point.

Every dial is painted and finished by hand, so the spacing, texture and character of the marks will naturally vary from watch to watch. The overall design remains recognisable, but no two examples will ever be completely identical.

Underneath the artwork is the Citizen Tsuyosa Automatic, chosen for its clean proportions, integrated stainless-steel bracelet and dependable automatic movement. The case, bracelet, movement and functional elements of the watch are carefully handled throughout the customisation process before the finished dial is reassembled into the watch.

The Off Dial story

Serious watches.
Less serious dials.

We have fun with the dial. We do not mess about with the process. Each design follows a careful multi-stage approach shaped by its complexity: intricate compositions can begin with precision artwork before hand painting, while simpler designs are painted directly by hand. Every dial is individually worked, hand finished and carefully reassembled by hand.

Watches can be precise without being predictable. We take familiar, genuine watches and give the dial permission to misbehave a little. The colour, texture and tiny details are where the personality lives; the care taken around the watch is where our standards live.

Offbeat by design.

Careful by nature.

From genuine watch to individual artwork

Seven careful stages.
One very Off Dial result.

01

Start with the real thing.

Every Off Dial begins with a genuine watch. Its case, movement and bracelet already have an identity; the dial is where we get to have some fun. Before any work starts, the watch is inspected and its original components are handled and retained with care.

02

Open it properly.

This is not the bit for improvisation. The watch is carefully opened so we can reach the dial, with the hands, dial and surrounding components removed and kept safely together for reassembly. The goal is simple: give the artwork room to happen without treating the watch casually.

03

Prep the tiny canvas.

The original dial is cleaned and prepared for the layers to come. At this scale, a millimetre can change the whole composition, so placement, colour and the relationship between the artwork and the watch’s existing details are considered before the fun starts.

04

Let the design call the shots.

The complexity of the artwork shapes how we begin. For intricate compositions, precision artwork is laid onto the prepared dial to establish the design before it is developed through hand painting. For simpler compositions, the artwork is painted directly onto the dial by hand.

Both approaches leave room for the artist’s individual touch. Colour, texture and fine detail are worked by hand, giving each finished dial its own character.

05

Now the fun part.

Hand painting is at the heart of Off Dial. Colour is developed, texture is built and fine details are worked into each dial individually. A highlight can change the movement of a wave; a deeper blue can change its depth. Every brushstroke adds something particular to the piece in front of the artist.

Even when two dials begin with the same design, the handwork takes each in its own direction. The texture, the colour and the smallest details vary from dial to dial. Those differences are part of what makes it an Off Dial.

06

Sweat the small stuff.

Each dial is refined and finished by hand, with attention to the artwork, its edges and the details that bring the whole composition together. Indices and dial details are carefully attended to before reassembly. Playful does not mean slapdash; the small stuff matters.

07

Put it back together. Properly.

The finished dial returns to its watch and the components are carefully reassembled by hand. The familiar shape remains, now carrying artwork with its own colour, texture and attitude. An Off Dial is made to be worn, lived with and enjoyed — not just looked at from a safe distance.
